Privacy Policy.

This page explains the intended privacy practices for the Access Soft Labs website.

Effective date: 20 October 2026. This is a starter policy for the current static website. The site owner must review and update it to reflect the actual hosting, analytics, advertising, forms and other services used before publication.

1. Who we are

Access Soft Labs (ASL) operates this website to share information about its services and provide ways to make contact. For privacy enquiries, use hello@accesssoftlabs.com after confirming this address is active.

2. Information you provide

The contact form on this starter website prepares an email in your device's email application. It does not submit the form to an ASL server. If you choose to send that email, ASL may receive the details you include, such as your name, email address, organisation and project message. Avoid including passwords, payment details or other sensitive information in an initial enquiry.

3. Technical information and hosting

Like most websites, the hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, timestamps and security logs. The exact information retained depends on the hosting provider and its settings. The site owner should add the hosting provider's name, retention periods and contact route here.

4. Cookies, analytics and advertising

This template uses browser local storage to remember that the visitor dismissed the privacy notice. It does not currently include Google Analytics or Google AdSense code. If analytics, advertising, embedded media or other third-party tools are added, update this policy to identify the providers, explain their purposes and link to relevant provider policies. Where required by applicable law, obtain consent before using non-essential cookies or similar technologies.

Third-party advertising providers, including Google if enabled, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure or personalise ads according to their policies and the visitor's settings. The site owner must use the current consent and disclosure requirements that apply to the site's audience and advertising setup.

5. How information may be used

Information sent by email may be used to reply to enquiries, discuss requested services, provide support and maintain business records. ASL should not use contact information for unrelated marketing without an appropriate legal basis or consent where required.

6. Sharing and retention

Information may be handled by service providers needed to run the business, such as email and website hosting providers. The site owner should retain information only as long as necessary for the purpose or applicable legal requirements and apply appropriate security measures.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information. Contact ASL using the email above to make a request. Identity verification may be necessary before acting on a request.

8. Children's privacy

This website is intended as a general business website, not as a service specifically directed to children. If the website begins collecting information from children or is directed to them, the owner must review the additional requirements that apply.

9. Security and policy changes

No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. We aim to use reasonable safeguards and may update this policy as the website and applicable requirements change. The current version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: hello@accesssoftlabs.com. Confirm this address and the policy details before the site goes live.
