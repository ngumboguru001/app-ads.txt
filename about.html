A LITTLE ABOUT US

Technology should open doors.

Access Soft Labs (ASL) is a digital solutions company focused on making technology practical, approachable and useful for organisations and people.

Founded in 2024, ASL began with a simple idea: make quality digital tools and design more accessible to businesses and organisations.

Our story

Access Soft Labs works across website development, custom software, mobile applications and digital design. We aim to understand the problem first, recommend a practical approach and build solutions that people can confidently use. Based in Zimbabwe, ASL has a global outlook and welcomes conversations with clients and collaborators from different markets.

Leadership

ASL is led by CEO Nyashadyashe Gumboguru. The company describes its development team as a 30-person team working across a range of digital skills. As projects and partnerships evolve, we focus on collaboration, clear communication and continuous improvement.
