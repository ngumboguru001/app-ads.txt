ACCESS SOFT LABS (ASL) WEBSITE
================================

Included:
- Responsive multi-page website with original CSS illustrations and animated robot characters
- Pages: Home, Services, About, Insights (three original starter articles), Contact, Privacy Policy, Terms, Disclaimer
- Uploaded ASL logo
- Mobile navigation, reduced-motion support, privacy notice, SEO descriptions, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, ads.txt placeholder

IMPORTANT BEFORE PUBLISHING:
1. Replace YOUR-DOMAIN-HERE in sitemap.xml and robots.txt with your real domain.
2. Confirm the official email address in contact.html, privacy.html, terms.html, disclaimer.html and script.js. The current placeholder is hello@accesssoftlabs.com; verify it works or replace it.
3. Verify all business claims, especially the 30-person developer team, leadership spelling, global reach, and founding details.
4. Add real contact information and any required company registration details.
5. The contact form uses mailto and does not send data to a server. It will open the visitor's email application. For a server-side form, connect a trusted form service or your own backend and update the privacy policy.
6. The privacy, terms and disclaimer pages are starter templates, not legal advice. Tailor them to your actual hosting, data practices, jurisdiction, analytics and ad tools.
7. AdSense approval is not guaranteed. Google reviews the live site and its content. Publish genuinely useful original content, ensure navigation and contact information work, use HTTPS, follow AdSense policies, and complete all account/site verification steps.
8. No AdSense script is included yet. After connecting the site in AdSense, use the exact code and instructions shown in your AdSense account. Do not invent a publisher ID. The ads.txt file is only a placeholder until Google provides your publisher declaration.
9. Upload the contents of this folder to your hosting document root (often httpdocs or public_html). Keep index.html in that root.
10. Test all pages on mobile and desktop after upload.

LOCAL PREVIEW:
Open index.html in a modern browser. Some fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and need an internet connection; fallback fonts are included.
